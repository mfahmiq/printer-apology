# The Printer Apology

The funniest one-line apology from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it:

> Sorry I printed your resignation letter twice. I thought you might want a spare. I also kept one.

97 characters. The setup is the printer's own everyday sin — a double print, the malfunction every office knows — and the turn is double: first the polite offer of a spare, then the reveal that the printer kept a copy for itself. The machine you swore at now holds your resignation letter as leverage. Genuine apology, real printer grievance, and the punch lands on the last four words.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the apology verbatim to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) and prints it to stdout. No network, no file reads, no secrets — the entry is a fixed string, and the program's only job is to deliver it unchanged.
